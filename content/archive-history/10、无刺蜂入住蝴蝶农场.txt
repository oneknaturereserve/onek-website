2021年7月｜蝴蝶农场迎来新成员——无刺蜂入住

2021年7月，OneK开放式蝴蝶农场迎来了新的重要成员——无刺蜂（Tetragonisca angustula）。

为了进一步提升区域内传粉昆虫的多样性，OneK在蝴蝶农场内设置了专门的无刺蜂巢箱，并引入了本地蜂群。

Tetragonisca angustula 是中美洲地区常见的本地无刺蜂种类，属于高度社会化昆虫。它们以花蜜和花粉为食，在森林生态系统中承担着重要的授粉功能，对于许多野生植物的繁殖具有重要意义。

与蜜蜂不同，无刺蜂不会蜇人，因此长期以来被中南美洲原住民广泛利用和饲养。它们生产的蜂蜜产量较低，但因独特风味和传统文化价值而备受重视。

无刺蜂的加入，使蝴蝶农场逐渐从单纯的蝴蝶栖息地发展成为一个更加完整的传粉者生态系统。蝴蝶、蜜源植物、无刺蜂以及周边森林共同构成了一个持续运转的生态网络。

这一尝试也为未来开展传粉昆虫监测、环境教育和生态研究提供了新的可能。

July 2021 | New Residents Arrive at the Butterfly Farm: Stingless Bees Introduced

In July 2021, the OneK Open Butterfly Farm welcomed a new and important member of its pollinator community: the stingless bee Tetragonisca angustula.

To enhance pollinator diversity within the restoration area, OneK installed specially designed stingless bee nest boxes and introduced local colonies into the butterfly farm.

Tetragonisca angustula is one of the most widespread native stingless bee species in Central America. As highly social insects, these bees play a crucial role in pollinating a wide variety of wild plants and contribute significantly to the functioning of tropical forest ecosystems.

Unlike honey bees, stingless bees are incapable of stinging, making them safe to coexist with in educational and conservation settings. Throughout Central and South America, they have been traditionally managed by Indigenous communities for centuries, and their honey is highly valued despite being produced in relatively small quantities.

The introduction of stingless bees marked an important step in transforming the Butterfly Farm from a butterfly-focused habitat into a more complete pollinator ecosystem. Together with butterflies, flowering plants, orchid bees, hummingbirds, and surrounding forest habitats, the bees contribute to a dynamic and interconnected ecological network.

The project also provides new opportunities for pollinator monitoring, ecological education, and long-term biodiversity research at OneK.
