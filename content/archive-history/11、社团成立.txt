2024年5月｜Natural Echo 自然教育社团成立

2024年5月，由 Haoming（虫虫）和 Keying（蝶儿）发起的青少年自然教育社团 Natural Echo 在 OneK 正式成立。

从小跟随家人走访世界各地的自然生态热点地区，并在哥斯达黎加雨林中成长，他们在长期的自然观察与实践中逐渐意识到，保护自然不仅需要科学研究和保护行动，同样需要让更多年轻人认识自然、理解自然。

带着这样的想法，他们决定建立一个由青少年发起的自然教育平台，希望通过分享自己的观察经历和自然知识，帮助更多儿童和青少年走进自然，发现生命世界的精彩。

Natural Echo 成立后，陆续开展观鸟活动、自然观察、生态科普和环境教育等公益活动，向更多孩子分享关于鸟类、昆虫、两栖动物以及热带雨林生态系统的知识。

对于 OneK 而言，Natural Echo 的成立不仅意味着一个新的教育项目诞生，更代表着保护理念开始向下一代传递。

因为真正长久的保护，不仅来自今天的行动，也来自未来一代对自然的理解与热爱。

今天，Natural Echo 已成为连接自然与青少年的桥梁，让保护的声音在更多年轻人的心中持续回响。


May 2024 | Natural Echo is Founded at OneK

In May 2024, Natural Echo, a youth-led nature education initiative founded by Haoming and Keying, was officially established at OneK.

Having grown up exploring some of the world’s most remarkable biodiversity hotspots and later spending their formative years in the rainforests of Costa Rica, they gradually came to realize that protecting nature requires more than scientific research and conservation action—it also requires inspiring the next generation to understand and care about the natural world.

With this belief in mind, they created Natural Echo as a platform led by young people, for young people. Their goal was simple: to share their experiences, knowledge, and passion for nature with other children and teenagers, encouraging them to step outside, explore the natural world, and discover the wonder of biodiversity for themselves.

Since its founding, Natural Echo has organized a variety of free educational activities, including birdwatching walks, nature observation programs, environmental education workshops, and biodiversity outreach events. Through these activities, children have had opportunities to learn about birds, insects, amphibians, reptiles, and the unique ecosystems of tropical rainforests.

For OneK, the establishment of Natural Echo marked more than the launch of a new education initiative. It represented an important step in passing conservation values to the next generation.

Because lasting conservation depends not only on the actions we take today, but also on the understanding, curiosity, and stewardship of the young people who will shape tomorrow.

Today, Natural Echo continues to serve as a bridge between nature and youth, helping inspire a new generation to explore, appreciate, and protect the natural world.
