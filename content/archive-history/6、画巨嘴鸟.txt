2020年3月2日｜孩子们画下属于OneK的第一只巨嘴鸟

2020年3月2日，我们和孩子们一起完成了 OneK 营地的第一幅生态主题壁画。

壁画以 OneK 标志和红嘴簇舌巨嘴鸟（Keel-billed Toucan）为主题，绘制于营地主建筑外墙之上。这不仅是一次简单的绘画创作，更成为 OneK 发展历程中的一个特殊时刻。

从保护区建立之初，OneK 就不仅仅是一片森林。

它也是一个家庭共同成长、共同建设的地方。

在这幅壁画的创作过程中，孩子们亲手参与设计、勾线和上色。从最初的草图，到一点点完成羽毛、颜色和细节，每一个部分都凝聚着全家人的努力与热爱。

之所以选择红嘴簇舌巨嘴鸟作为主题，是因为它不仅是中美洲最具代表性的鸟类之一，也是许多人第一次来到热带雨林时最向往见到的物种之一。

鲜艳的色彩、独特的巨大嘴喙以及充满热带气息的形象，使它成为新热带雨林生物多样性的象征。

对于 OneK 而言，这幅壁画还有另一层意义。

它代表着我们希望将艺术与自然结合，让保护不只是科学监测和生态管理，也成为一种能够被看见、被感受和被分享的文化表达。

多年过去，这幅壁画依然保留在营地之中。

对于来访者而言，它是一幅关于热带雨林的艺术作品；而对于我们来说，它更像是一段被留在墙上的记忆。

它记录着 OneK 发展的早期岁月，也记录着一个家庭与这片森林共同成长的故事。

直到今天，每当经过这面墙，我们依然会想起那个下午。

森林就在身边，而未来正在一点点被描绘出来。

March 2, 2020 | A Family Creates OneK’s First Wildlife Mural

On March 2, 2020, our family completed the first wildlife-themed mural at OneK.

Featuring the OneK logo and a Keel-billed Toucan, the mural was painted on the exterior wall of the station’s main building. More than a piece of artwork, it became a lasting symbol of OneK’s early years and the family’s shared journey in building the reserve.

From the very beginning, OneK was never simply a protected area.

It was also a place where our family learned, explored, and grew together.

Throughout the project, the children participated in sketching, outlining, and painting the mural. From the first pencil marks to the final brushstrokes, every section reflected the enthusiasm, creativity, and dedication that helped shape OneK during its formative years.

The Keel-billed Toucan was chosen as the centerpiece because it is one of the most iconic birds of Central America and one of the species most closely associated with tropical rainforests. Its colorful plumage and unmistakable bill have made it a symbol of Neotropical biodiversity around the world.

For OneK, the mural carries a deeper meaning.

It represents the connection between art and conservation, reminding us that protecting nature is not only about research and management, but also about creating stories, memories, and cultural connections that inspire people to care about the natural world.

Years later, the mural still stands at the station.

For visitors, it is a colorful image of rainforest wildlife.

For our family, it is a reminder of a special chapter in OneK’s history—a time when a dream was still taking shape and every part of the station was being built by hand.

It remains a lasting symbol of the bond between a family and a rainforest, and of the belief that conservation begins with connection.
