2020年7月3日｜发现非法狩猎活动，加强保护区巡护

2020年7月3日，OneK保护区在例行检查中发现有猎人非法进入保护区活动。

在此次事件中，野外布设的两台红外相机遭到人为破坏。红外相机长期用于监测保护区内野生动物活动情况，也是开展生物多样性调查的重要设备。

事件发生后，OneK进一步加强了保护区巡护频率，并持续开展反盗猎巡查工作。同时在重点区域增加监测设备布设，以提高对非法狩猎活动的发现和预警能力。

这次事件也提醒我们，雨林保护不仅仅是记录野生动物，更需要持续的巡护与守护。对于许多野生动物而言，保护栖息地和减少人为干扰同样重要。




July 3, 2020 | Illegal Hunting Activity Detected, Patrol Efforts Strengthened

On July 3, 2020, signs of unauthorized hunting activity were detected within the OneK Reserve during a routine field inspection.

During the incident, two camera traps deployed for wildlife monitoring were deliberately damaged. These camera traps play an important role in documenting biodiversity and monitoring wildlife activity throughout the reserve.

Following the event, OneK strengthened its patrol program and increased anti-poaching surveillance efforts. Additional monitoring equipment was deployed in key areas to improve the detection of illegal activities and enhance protection measures.

The incident served as an important reminder that rainforest conservation involves more than documenting wildlife—it also requires continuous stewardship and on-the-ground protection. For many species, safeguarding habitat and reducing human disturbance are essential components of effective conservation.
