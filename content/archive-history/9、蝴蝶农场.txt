2020年11月｜建立 OneK 开放式蝴蝶农场

2020年11月，OneK在此前完成蕨类植物清理和生态恢复的区域内，建立了一个开放式（Open Butterfly Farm）蝴蝶农场。

与传统封闭式蝴蝶园不同，OneK并未使用围网或人工饲养设施，而是通过种植大量本地蝴蝶寄主植物（Host Plants）和蜜源植物（Nectar Plants），主动营造适合蝴蝶繁殖和取食的自然栖息环境。

项目的目标并非人工饲养蝴蝶，而是吸引野生蝴蝶自然进入、产卵、完成幼虫发育并形成稳定种群，使整个区域成为一个能够自我维持的天然蝴蝶繁殖基地。

随着植被逐渐恢复，越来越多的蝴蝶开始利用这些植物资源。寄主植物为幼虫提供食物来源，而丰富的花蜜资源则为成蝶提供能量补给，形成完整的生命周期支持系统。

这一尝试不仅提升了当地传粉昆虫多样性，也为未来开展蝴蝶监测、生态教育和昆虫多样性研究提供了重要基础。

November 2020 | Establishment of the OneK Open Butterfly Farm

In November 2020, OneK established an Open Butterfly Farm within an area undergoing ecological restoration following the removal of dense fern cover.

Unlike conventional butterfly gardens that rely on enclosures or captive breeding, the OneK Butterfly Farm was designed as a completely open and natural habitat. A diverse collection of native butterfly host plants and nectar-producing plants was introduced to attract wild butterflies and support their entire life cycle.

The objective was not to raise butterflies artificially, but to create suitable habitat conditions that would encourage wild butterflies to lay eggs, allow caterpillars to develop naturally, and ultimately establish self-sustaining populations within the restored landscape.

As vegetation recovered, increasing numbers of butterfly species began utilizing the area. Host plants provide food resources for caterpillars, while nectar plants supply essential energy for adult butterflies, creating a complete ecological support system for reproduction and survival.

The project contributes to pollinator conservation, enhances local insect biodiversity, and provides valuable opportunities for long-term butterfly monitoring, ecological education, and biodiversity research at OneK.
