2019年8月22日｜开展首次反偷猎清理行动

2019年8月22日，OneK组织当地社区成员在保护区内开展了一次大规模的反偷猎巡查行动。

在此前的日常巡护过程中，我们发现保护区内存在猎人活动留下的痕迹。为了减少野生动物面临的威胁，我们联合当地人员对保护区进行了全面排查，重点寻找和清理猎人设置的各类捕猎陷阱。

行动当天，队伍沿着森林步道、溪流周边以及动物活动频繁的区域进行系统搜索。巡查过程中发现并清除了多处用于捕捉野生动物的陷阱和装置。

这些陷阱虽然看似简单，却可能对哺乳动物、鸟类以及其他野生动物造成严重伤害，甚至导致死亡。对于许多大型动物而言，一个隐藏在森林中的陷阱往往意味着致命风险。

除了清理陷阱之外，我们还在保护区边界的重要入口和通道设置了保护区标识牌，明确告知来访者和周边居民：

禁止狩猎（No Hunting）
禁止设置陷阱（No Trapping）
禁止未经授权进入保护区开展捕猎活动。

这些标识不仅是边界的提醒，更代表着保护区对野生动物和自然生态系统的长期承诺。

虽然这次行动规模并不庞大，但它标志着 OneK 开始建立更加主动的保护管理体系。从被动记录自然，到主动守护自然，保护工作迈出了重要的一步。

此后，多年的持续巡护、反偷猎管理以及社区沟通工作逐渐成为 OneK 保护行动的重要组成部分。随着保护力度不断加强，保护区内野生动物活动记录也逐年增加，为生态系统恢复提供了更加安全的环境。

回顾今天，这次行动不仅仅是一次陷阱清理工作。

它是 OneK 首次以实际行动向外界传递一个明确的信息：

这片森林属于野生动物。

这里不欢迎猎枪，也不欢迎陷阱。

这里是它们的家园。

August 22, 2019 | OneK Conducts Its First Anti-Poaching Patrol

On August 22, 2019, OneK organized a large-scale anti-poaching patrol within the reserve together with members of the local community.

During routine field patrols, evidence of hunting activity had been discovered within the forest. To reduce threats to wildlife, a coordinated effort was launched to search for and remove traps and other hunting devices throughout the reserve.

The patrol team systematically surveyed forest trails, stream corridors, and areas frequently used by wildlife. Multiple traps and hunting devices were located and removed during the operation.

Although often simple in design, these traps can cause severe injuries or death to mammals, birds, and other wildlife. Hidden within the forest, they represent a significant threat to the ecological integrity of protected areas.

In addition to removing traps, OneK installed protected-area signs along key access points and boundary routes surrounding the reserve. The signs clearly communicated the reserve’s conservation regulations:

No Hunting
No Trapping
No Unauthorized Entry for Hunting Activities

These signs served not only as boundary markers but also as a public declaration of the reserve’s commitment to wildlife protection.

While modest in scale, this operation marked an important milestone in OneK’s conservation history. It represented the transition from simply observing nature to actively protecting it.

In the years that followed, regular patrols, anti-poaching efforts, and community engagement became integral components of OneK’s conservation strategy. As protection measures strengthened, wildlife observations and biodiversity records within the reserve continued to increase, reflecting a safer environment for native species.

Looking back, this was more than a trap-removal operation.

It was OneK’s first clear statement that the forest would be protected.

A declaration that this land exists first and foremost as a refuge for wildlife.

A place where traps and hunting have no place.

A place where nature is given the opportunity to thrive.
