2018年4月10日｜OneK 的开始

2018年4月10日｜一切开始的地方

2018年4月10日，我们第一次亲手打开了通往这片雨林的大门。

这一天，我们正式完成土地交割，获得了这片雨林的所有权。从法律意义上来说，这是一笔土地交易；但对我们而言，它更像是一段漫长旅程的开始。

站在森林入口，我们并不知道未来会发生什么。

那时的 OneK 还不是保护区，也没有生物站、科研项目或生态教育计划。这里没有完善的道路，没有观测平台，没有监测体系，更没有今天所积累的物种记录与保护成果。

它只是一片安静而原始的热带雨林。

而我们，只是因为热爱自然，决定守护这片土地。

最初的想法其实很简单。我们希望拥有一片属于自己的雨林，希望能够近距离观察和记录这里丰富而神奇的生命。那时的我们并没有明确的方向，也没有宏大的规划，只是怀着对自然的好奇与热爱，迈出了第一步。

随着时间的推移，这片森林逐渐改变了我们。

我们开始认识这里的鸟类、昆虫、两栖动物和哺乳动物；开始记录物种，修复河流，保护森林；开始建立长期监测项目，开展环境教育活动，并与越来越多热爱自然的人分享这片雨林的故事。

从最初的一片森林，到今天的 OneK 自然保护区与生物站，所有的一切都源于这一天。

回望过去，2018年4月10日不仅仅是获得土地所有权的日子。

它是 OneK 故事的第一页。

也是一个家庭与一片雨林共同成长旅程的开始。

直到今天，每一次走进森林，每一次记录生命，每一次为保护自然而付出的努力，都与这一天紧紧相连。


April 10, 2018｜The Day OneK Began

On April 10, 2018, we opened the gate to this rainforest for the very first time as its new stewards.

After months of preparation, paperwork, and planning, our family officially completed the purchase of the property and received ownership of the land. What stood before us was not a biological station, a conservation project, or a long-term research site.

It was simply a piece of rainforest.

At the time, we did not have a detailed roadmap for the future. We only knew that we loved nature and wanted to protect a place where wildlife could continue to thrive.

The forest we entered that day was very different from what visitors see today. Trails were limited, infrastructure was nonexistent, and much of the property remained unexplored. Every stream, ridge, and patch of forest represented a new discovery waiting to happen.

What began as a personal dream gradually evolved into something much larger.

Over the years, the rainforest became the foundation for biodiversity surveys, wildlife monitoring, habitat restoration, environmental education, and scientific collaboration. Thousands of species observations, countless hours of fieldwork, and an ever-growing commitment to conservation all trace back to this single day.

Looking back, April 10, 2018 was more than a property transaction.

It was the first chapter of the OneK story.

A story of a family and a rainforest growing together.

Today, OneK continues to protect and study this remarkable ecosystem, but every step forward still leads back to the moment when the gate was opened for the first time and a lifelong journey began.
