2019年5月｜发现OneK地下泉眼


2019年5月｜在对保护区进行日常调查和探索的过程中，我们发现了一处常年持续出水的地下泉眼。

这处泉眼位于保护区内部山坡区域，即使在旱季期间仍保持稳定出水。对于刚刚开始建设的OneK来说，这一发现具有重要意义。

当时，营地尚未建立完善的供水系统，生活用水和未来基础设施建设都面临着现实挑战。寻找可靠的水源，也成为保护区建设过程中最重要的任务之一。

地下泉眼的发现，为这一问题提供了答案。

雨林中的生命之源

在热带雨林中，水不仅关系到人类活动，更是整个生态系统运转的基础。

泉眼的持续出水意味着地下含水层能够稳定补给地表水源，也反映出周边森林生态系统保持着良好的自然状态。

对于OneK而言，这处泉眼不仅是一处自然资源，更成为未来保护区发展的重要基础。

经过后续调查和评估，我们决定以这处泉眼作为核心水源，规划建设保护区内部供水系统。

从泉眼到供水系统

此后几年间，OneK逐步建设引水管道、储水设施以及供水网络。

这套系统为营地日常运行提供了稳定水源，也减少了对外部资源的依赖。

更重要的是，它让OneK能够以较低环境影响的方式开展长期保护工作。

直到今天，这处泉眼依然持续为保护区提供水源，支持着营地运行、科研监测和巡护工作的开展。

一次改变未来的发现

回顾OneK的发展历程，地下泉眼的发现或许不像道路建设那样显眼，也不像科研发现那样引人关注。

但它却深刻影响了保护区后来的发展。

许多后来得以实现的项目——营地建设、长期驻守、科研监测以及生态保护工作——都建立在稳定供水这一基础之上。

对于OneK来说，这处泉眼不仅是一处自然水源。

它也是这片森林赠予我们的第一份礼物。

May 2019｜Discovering the Spring That Would Sustain OneK

In May 2019, during routine exploration and surveys within the reserve, we discovered a perennial freshwater spring emerging from the hillside deep inside the forest.

Unlike seasonal streams that fluctuate throughout the year, this spring maintained a steady flow even during the dry season. For a young conservation project still in its early stages, the discovery proved to be one of the most important milestones in the development of OneK.

At the time, the reserve had no permanent water infrastructure. Securing a reliable water source was essential for future camp operations, conservation work, and long-term management of the property.

The spring provided that opportunity.

A Lifeline from the Forest

Water is the foundation of every ecosystem, and tropical rainforests are no exception.

The continuous flow of the spring indicated a healthy groundwater system and reflected the ecological integrity of the surrounding forest. Beyond its practical value, the spring represented a natural asset that could support the reserve while minimizing environmental impact.

Following additional evaluation, we decided that this spring would become the primary water source for the future OneK water supply system.

Building a Sustainable Water System

In the years that followed, the spring became the foundation of a gravity-fed water supply network within the reserve.

Pipelines, storage tanks, and distribution infrastructure were gradually installed, providing a dependable source of clean water for daily operations and long-term conservation activities.

By relying on a naturally flowing spring, OneK was able to reduce dependence on external water sources while maintaining a low-impact approach to infrastructure development.

Today, this spring continues to supply water to the reserve, supporting field research, conservation patrols, and life at the biological station.

A Discovery That Shaped the Future

Looking back, the discovery of the spring may seem like a small event compared to major construction projects or scientific achievements.

Yet it fundamentally changed what was possible at OneK.

Many of the initiatives that followed—including camp development, long-term monitoring programs, and year-round conservation work—were built upon the simple fact that a reliable source of water existed within the forest.

For us, the spring is more than a water source.

It is one of the first gifts the rainforest gave to OneK.
