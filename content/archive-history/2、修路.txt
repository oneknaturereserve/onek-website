2018年12月｜通往OneK保护区的道路建设


2018年12月｜OneK启动了通往保护区的道路建设工程。

在此之前，进入保护区十分困难。由于地处热带雨林山区，地形复杂，加之长期降雨，人员和物资运输都面临巨大挑战。营地建设所需的建筑材料、供水设施、监测设备以及日常生活物资，都需要依靠人工搬运进入森林。

对于一个刚刚起步的自然保护区来说，缺乏稳定的通行条件不仅增加了建设难度，也限制了后续保护工作的开展。

因此，我们决定修建一条通往保护区的道路。

为了进入森林，而不是开发森林

修建道路并不是为了改变森林，而是为了能够到达森林、守护森林。

在建设过程中，我们尽可能减少施工范围，利用现有地形条件，控制对周边植被和生态环境的影响。

对于OneK而言，这条道路承担着重要作用：

	•	支持保护区基础设施建设；
	•	保障生活和工作物资运输；
	•	提高巡护和管理效率；
	•	为未来科研和监测工作提供基础条件；
	•	提升紧急情况下的安全保障能力。

一切建设的开始

这条道路的建成，为OneK后续的发展奠定了基础。

此后几年间，营地建设逐步完善，供水系统得以建立，巡护工作持续开展，红外相机监测、生物多样性调查以及生态恢复项目也陆续启动。

可以说，后来发生在OneK的许多保护工作，都是从这条道路开始的。

通往未来的第一步

今天，当我们沿着这条道路进入保护区时，它看起来或许只是一条普通的山路。

但对于OneK来说，它承载着一段特殊的记忆。

它见证了一个家庭为了守护雨林所迈出的重要一步，也见证了OneK从一个想法逐渐成长为一座生物站和自然保护区的过程。

从某种意义上说，这条道路不仅连接了外界与森林。

它也连接了梦想与现实。

December 2018｜Building the Road to OneK

In December 2018, construction began on the access road leading to the OneK Nature Reserve.

At the time, reaching the property was one of the greatest challenges we faced. Located within the tropical rainforest of Costa Rica, the reserve was accessible only through difficult terrain, especially during the rainy season. Transporting building materials, equipment, supplies, and daily necessities required significant time and effort, often relying on manual labor.

For a newly established conservation project, the lack of reliable access not only slowed development but also limited our ability to carry out effective protection and management activities.

To address this challenge, we began constructing a road that would provide safe and sustainable access to the reserve.

Reaching the Forest to Protect It

The purpose of the road was never to develop the rainforest.

Its purpose was to make conservation possible.

Throughout the project, we worked to minimize environmental impacts by following the natural terrain as much as possible and limiting unnecessary disturbance to surrounding vegetation.

For OneK, this road became essential for:

	•	Transporting construction materials and equipment;
	•	Supporting daily reserve management and patrol activities;
	•	Improving safety and emergency access;
	•	Facilitating future scientific research and biodiversity monitoring;
	•	Providing the infrastructure needed for long-term conservation efforts.

The Foundation of Everything That Followed

The completion of the access road marked a turning point in the development of OneK.

It enabled the construction of the camp, the installation of water systems, the expansion of conservation patrols, and the implementation of long-term ecological monitoring programs.

In the years that followed, camera trap projects, biodiversity surveys, habitat restoration initiatives, and scientific research activities all became possible because the reserve could finally be accessed more effectively.

Many of the conservation achievements at OneK can trace their beginnings back to this first infrastructure project.

The First Step Toward a Bigger Vision

Today, the road may appear to be nothing more than a simple mountain track leading into the rainforest.

But for OneK, it represents far more.

It symbolizes the first major step taken by our family toward building a place dedicated to conservation, research, and environmental stewardship.

In many ways, this road does not simply connect the outside world to the forest.

It connects a dream to reality.
