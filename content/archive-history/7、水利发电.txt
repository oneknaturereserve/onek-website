2020年4月｜OneK微型水力发电系统试验

2020年4月，为了解决雨林深处供电困难的问题，OneK开展了一次小型水力发电系统试验，希望探索一种更加可持续、低碳的能源解决方案，为未来保护区的长期运营提供支持。

当时，营地刚刚完成初步建设，电力供应仍是最大的挑战之一。考虑到保护区内拥有常年流动的山溪，我们尝试利用溪流水位落差建设一套微型引水式水力发电系统，通过自然水流带动发电机运转，为营地提供基础电力。

项目建设过程中，我们铺设了引水管道，并安装了小型水轮发电设备。这是OneK首次尝试将可再生能源应用于保护区管理和野外基础设施建设之中。

一次重要的实践与探索

在试运行期间，系统成功实现了发电目标，但随着进一步测试，我们也发现了一系列现实问题。

热带雨林复杂的地形条件、季节性水量变化、设备维护难度以及长期运行稳定性等因素，都给系统持续运行带来了挑战。

经过约两周的实际运行和综合评估后，我们最终决定停止该项目，并未将其作为保护区长期供电方案继续建设。

虽然这次尝试没有取得预期成果，但它为OneK积累了宝贵的经验，也让我们更加深入地理解了在热带雨林环境中建设可再生能源系统所面临的技术和生态挑战。

保护与发展的平衡

OneK始终坚持一个原则：

任何基础设施建设，都应尽可能减少对生态环境的干扰，同时满足长期保护工作的实际需求。

微型水电项目的实践，让我们认识到，在雨林环境中，能源系统不仅需要具备稳定性和可持续性，还必须兼顾对生态系统的影响以及后期维护成本。

这次经历也促使我们开始关注其他更加适合保护区发展的清洁能源方案，其中包括未来可能建设的太阳能供电系统。

每一次尝试都值得记录

保护工作从来不是一条预先规划好的道路。

很多时候，我们需要不断尝试、不断学习，再根据实际情况调整方向。

微型水力发电项目虽然最终没有被保留下来，但它仍然是OneK发展历程中的重要一页。

它记录着我们对可持续发展的探索，也记录着OneK在建设初期不断摸索、不断成长的过程。

今天回望这段经历，我们依然认为：

真正重要的并不是一次项目是否成功，而是在每一次实践中获得的经验与成长。

April 2020｜Testing a Small Hydropower System in the Rainforest

In April 2020, OneK carried out an experimental project to explore renewable energy solutions for operating in a remote rainforest environment.

At the time, access to reliable electricity remained one of the biggest challenges for maintaining basic infrastructure and supporting future conservation work within the reserve. In an effort to reduce dependence on fossil fuels, we tested a small run-of-stream hydropower system using water from a nearby forest stream.

The project involved installing a temporary pipeline and micro-hydropower unit designed to generate electricity from the natural flow of water.

Learning from the Rainforest

Although the system successfully operated during initial testing, several practical challenges quickly became apparent.

The steep terrain, seasonal fluctuations in stream flow, maintenance requirements, and the long-term reliability of the system made it difficult to sustain under the conditions present at OneK.

After approximately two weeks of operation and evaluation, the project was discontinued.

While the experiment did not become a permanent solution, it provided valuable experience and a deeper understanding of the technical and environmental considerations involved in implementing renewable energy systems in tropical rainforest environments.

Conservation with Minimal Impact

OneK has always sought ways to balance human needs with environmental protection.

The hydropower trial reinforced an important principle:

Infrastructure within a protected area should minimize ecological disturbance while remaining practical and sustainable for long-term conservation work.

The lessons learned from this project later contributed to discussions about alternative renewable energy options, including solar power systems that may offer lower maintenance requirements and reduced impacts on sensitive rainforest habitats.

Every Attempt Matters

Not every conservation initiative becomes a permanent success.

However, each attempt provides knowledge that helps shape better decisions for the future.

The 2020 hydropower experiment remains an important chapter in OneK’s development—an example of our willingness to test new ideas, learn from experience, and continually seek more sustainable ways to support conservation in the rainforest.
